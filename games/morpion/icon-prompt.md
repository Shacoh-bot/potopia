# Illustration de la tuile Morpion

Générée avec l'outil intégré de génération d'images, puis exportée en WebP 480 × 480 dans
`apps/web/public/apps/morpion.webp`. Références : illustration Agents Doubles et capture des tuiles
du portail fournies dans la conversation. Le registre des applications utilise cette image sur l'accueil.

## Prompt initial

Create ONE finished square game launcher tile for the new MORPION (tic-tac-toe) game, matching exactly the illustrated arcade thumbnail family in the reference images. Reference 1 is an existing tile for illustration style, thick ink outlines, polished vivid cartoon shading, typography hierarchy and rounded glowing frame. Reference 2 shows the family and the empty Morpion slot; do NOT reproduce the screenshot or other tiles. Output a single square 1024x1024 icon filling the canvas, no outside margins. Central character: the same friendly bald bespectacled middle-aged man with short dark goatee, black rectangular glasses, navy blazer, white shirt, small playful smile, shown in three-quarter view leaning over a spectacular glowing 3x3 tic-tac-toe board; he is placing a chunky luminous golden X, with cyan O pieces on the board. Clearly show a legal clean 3x3 board. Make this character look like the family mascot rather than a new person. Electric blue and magenta arcade background, golden victory sparkles, dynamic cyan light streaks, high contrast and saturation, glossy game illustration, rounded neon cyan/magenta outer frame with very thin gold inner accent. Lower third typography: very large bold italic white word "MORPION" with dark navy extrusion and outline, centered and perfectly legible at small size. Under a thin gold rule at the bottom, a short small uppercase white French subtitle EXACTLY "ALIGNEZ 3 PIONS !". Match the consistent title and footer arrangement of reference 1. No extra words, no names of other games, no logos, no additional panels. The artwork must read crisply as a small game tile beside the references.

## Direction retenue

À la demande de l'utilisateur, rapprochement du dessin de la tuile « Boîte à idées » : cadre doré,
fond violet, dessin simplifié et titre blanc et jaune. Correction de la grille pour trois lignes
et trois colonnes. L'illustration corrigée sert de référence à la dernière retouche ci-dessous.

## Prompt final de retouche

Edit only the mascot's clothing and facial hair in this exact Morpion game tile. Replace the navy business suit, white shirt, tie and white cuffs with a casual dark navy blue HOODIE sweatshirt, visibly with a soft hood behind his neck, simple drawstrings and ribbed cuffs, no logos, no tie, no formal lapels. Add a clearly visible thick BLACK MOUSTACHE above his smiling mouth, separate from and in addition to his existing small dark goatee. Keep his bald head, black rectangular glasses, friendly smiling expression, head size and hand positions. Preserve absolutely everything else unchanged: cartoon drawing style, proportions, gold X held up in his hand, the proper THREE BY THREE nine-cell tic-tac-toe board with its two horizontal and two vertical internal lines and exact current pieces, scattered X/O symbols, purple background, thin rounded glowing gold frame, white/yellow title MORPION and footer ALIGNEZ 3 PIONS !. Same square composition, no extra text, polished final icon.
